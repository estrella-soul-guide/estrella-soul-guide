Regression Therapy

A Channeled Regression from your teams of Light guided by your Soul. A beautiful way to heal trauma and work with your shadow.

In Regression Therapy we take a deep dive into the Akashic Records, we journey into your body, and we use various methods such as Parts Therapy, timeline viewing, and information from your higher self to work through deep trauma and even do Soul Retrieval.

Through regression, we can dive deep into your past and gain a deeper understanding of the events, experiences, and relationships that have shaped your life. This process of self awareness empowers you to discover hidden aspects of yourself, connect the dots between past and present, and gain insights into your behaviour, emotions, and patterns of thinking.

Unresolved emotional wounds and traumas from the past can continue to impact our lives in profound ways. Regression therapy provides a safe and supportive space to explore and heal these deeper wounds. By revisiting past experiences and allowing suppressed emotions to surface, you can release pain, find closure, and experience profound healing and emotional freedom.

Limiting beliefs and patterns that hold us back from reaching our full potential can be deeply ingrained in our subconscious minds. Regression therapy helps identify and transform these limiting beliefs by bringing them to conscious awareness.

The Akashic Records or “The Book of Life” contains the entire history of every soul since the dawn of Creation. It holds every action, word, feeling, thought, and intent that an individual has ever had. The Akashic Records are interactive in that they have a tremendous influence upon our everyday lives, our relationships, our feelings and belief systems, and the potential realities we create. The Akashic Records attempt to guide, educate, and transform every individual so they can access their divine gifts and truly live their life purpose.

This Therapy works with the subconscious to uncover your deepest wounds and heal trauma quickly.
