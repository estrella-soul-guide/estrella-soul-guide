Your Body as the Ascension Vehicle

Genevieve IveyDecember 8, 20244 min read

The human body is an intelligent force that is the fabric of the universe. It doesn't mimic the essence of creation. It is the very substance of all that is. And thus it holds the wisdom and nature of creation itself.

During your soul's journey of remembering, your body will start to awaken with you. It will become your best friend, your constant companion that holds the wisdom that guides you. Your Divine Blueprint for Ascension will start to anchor and merge with your body and you will realize your body houses Divinity, Wisdom and the map of your journey. The manual you seek lies within you. The scared technology that your body IS reveals itself more and more to you. This is your awakening. To experience yourself as a soul in a sacred vessel. A vessel that isn't a reflection of God, of Creation, of the Earth you live IN, but an extension of all that is. Your body becomes sacred to you. You care for and are the keeper of this Sacred technology that carries your soul on its journey through life. And with this responsibility comes the desire for Autonomy and Sovereignty that includes, mind, body and soul, spiritualized and living as all it has the potential to become and already is. This is part of the descension of Soul into the Body. Of Sacred Soul Embodiment. Of living and walking in an awakened state of what it truly means to have the experience of being part of this Earth Family. Spiritual essence is a living breathing sentience that should be housed within all of existence. When the body becomes spiritualized it is resurrected as the House of God, of the Creator itself, as an extension of such. The body begins to let go of illusion, distortion, darkness, untruth and return to its natural state of oneness within all of creation. Just as you do during your awakening and Ascension process. Your body begins to live its multidimensional purpose as your Ascension Vehicle. A design long forgotten on Earth. Your body awakens to its Divinity when you awaken. And when you are ready you begin to Surrender to your soul's journey in a sacred vessel of vast intelligence you will start to feel what you actually live inside of. When your ego finally loses its grip on the Separation Game and you allow yourself to experience the Brilliance of what your truly are. A multidimensional being living in a Sacred vessel that can move beyond time and space and contains infinite wisdom about your relationships, your purpose, and your potential. Your journey will truly begin and you can Ascend into the Divine Matrix that is filled with the God code. Your body- your Ascension Vehicle knows the way. It knows how to take you there. Where to go. Your body knows exactly how to Ascend even when your mind doesn't.

Start to build deep connection and understanding with your body. Start to build true communication and deep loving relationship with your body. Start to awaken to the truth that your body has the map and knows exactly where to go. It's been guiding you all along. Trying to talk to you. Screaming at you when you won't listen. "You are off path! This hurts! You are going against your Soul's Plan! You are killing yourself with these thoughts! You can't eat that food, It lowers your vibration! You cant use that toxic product, it makes you sick!" Your body is constantly talking to you. This type of communication is surface level in comparison to the deep wisdom and guidance your body holds for you when you build connection with your body.

Your body loves you and its trying to guide you. You have a contract with your body for this lifetime. Your body is lovingly housing your soul and is ready to upgrade and hold more and more of your souls light and energies! Ascension and Descension are a journey that takes lifetimes to accomplish. Recognize your body as the Vehicle that gets you there and your journey will amaze you as you start to live your soul's purpose and experience multidimensional living in the Sacred Vessel of living breathing intelligence beyond what you can imagine!
