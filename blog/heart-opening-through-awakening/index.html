Heart Opening through Awakening

Genevieve IveyFebruary 14, 20262 min read

Shakti Speaks to me today about Heart Transformation. She is showing me the veils of the heart and I can feel the heaviness a veil creates in the heart. The heart can have endless veils but the fire of Kundalini can burn through all of them. Kundalini is your birthright. The forces of nature are meant to be continuously integrated into life experience. Kundalini is a force of connection. There is no spiritual bypassing with the Goddess. Your 3rd eye or the complex of glands that create spiritual sight, is purified by Shakti during a Kundalini Journey. Your heart has endless potential and in times past we are taught that our heart is an embodiment point for our soul and consciousness. But the new teachings of the Goddess are coming online and we are going deeper. The seat of the soul is the womb. For your soul to sit in the womb it means you have become your most primal self and then the true journey towards divinity can ensue. This is because divinity can't be experienced without experiencing the self. The process of discovering the self can be at times exhausting to say the least. In the true knowledge and wisdom of the Goddess the journey is Within. When love, thought, feeling, connection and direction come from deep within the womb we only see truth. Why? Because our fire is ignited and burning brightly and consistently from within the womb, keeping the heart light and strong. In Spiritual Bypassing we are focused on the heart and mind but true wisdom and connection come from deep within, bringing the body to life. The mind should be one with the body. Your thoughts should come from your wholeness, from oneness not your mind. After awakening you have zero thoughts and it is the most liberating and freeing experience to be free from the mind. Now you can start to live from the place of EVERYTHING instead of the polarity of the mind. The heart becomes a portal for seeing clearly, staying open to potential. When we have veils convoluting the heart we stay shut down, protecting ourselves from hurt and pain. An open heart is open to potential not the options the mind thinks are available ~ That is a veil of the heart. The veils of the heart are removed through specific processes that happen through divine will with the forces of nature. Shakti is one such force.
