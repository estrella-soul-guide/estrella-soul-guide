Guide Communication

Genevieve IveyFebruary 9, 20267 min read

I wanted to write about Clairvoyance and the higher deeper expression of this spiritual gift but my guides urged me instead to write about guide communication and to launch a class to teach and purify the Clairs. In 2015 my clairaudience turned on overnight. Instantly I could hear my guides talking to me. Directing me. Explaining things in terms I shouldn't be able to understand but somehow I knew exactly what they were talking about. Getting used to having what felt like a constant "comm" between me and my guides took years. Yes there was interference and astral entities to navigate, but ultimately my world opened up and I learned and still learn so much from my teams in the soul planes. What I learned was Spiritual gifts MUST be purified. Otherwise you WILL filter information and reframe things from your limited "self".

Before my Clairaudience turned on and I began the process of purifying and integrating information from other dimensional unseen realms, a lot of information would come through dreams or visions. I have also ALWAYS been strongly Clairsentient. Let's face it, I am bombarded by tons of information outside of the 5 senses. But that's just it, the information needs to be embodied and seen, felt, heard, touched even smelled in the 3d. It needs to be anchored into this dimension of time and reality. You need to drop density and anchor into the physical. On top of this you need to learn to stop filtering information with your mind. Surrender is a HUGE part of growth both spiritually and with guide communication because your mind wants to control the information by knowing it. That's a confusing sentence. But Soul is about experience not knowing. Soul is about being not doing. Quantum living isn't about dates, times, and creating outcomes. It's about experiencing the many possibilities uninhibited by doing and thinking. This is the space that our guides are communicating to us from. The space of limitless possibility. A place of oneness and defined frequency. We fear limitless possibility because of our conditioning and ego. We perceive knowing as safety. The reason the present moment is true reality, the only thing that actually exists, is because everything is always changing, second by second your reality is being created. Our survival ego creates veils that create illusion and distortion and we filter our reality through these veils. We all have them. During Spiritual Awakening these veils begin to dissolve and the ego is exposed. We begin to see the illusion we and everyone around us exists in. Before Spiritual awakening our gifts give us insight into into what our guides see from their more expansive view point. It's helpful because we are too close to observe our experience from a place a limitless perspective but also we are looking through the veil of our ego and often times it's a wounded ego that perceives many distortions as truth. Guide communication helps us see beyond the wounded ego.

During awakening we enter into states of purification and ego is constantly challenged by the Soul. Your reality becomes lucid and you see how it's constantly being created moment by moment. You are in the dream and you are trying to wake up. You are somewhere in between. A world within a world. Somewhere between awake and asleep. Communication with ourselves is critical but just as convoluted during awakening because there is no fixed self in Awakening. This is when we find a home within our bodies without the mind. We know our souls song, our souls signature and we stay there. In our souls signature with our Breath and with God, communicating with a deeper source of wisdom from within without all of the noise from the outer world that becomes so loud and chaotic during awakening. During this time our guides, god, our souls come to us to offer advice, guidance, the next step and information about out processes. Guide Communication helps purify and clarify these conversations by making them stronger, louder and unfiltered.

A Seerer is someone who has dissolved the ego, healed the wounds of the subpersonalities and sees beyond the veils of the illusion we all exist in. Clairvoyance is the main Clair at play but all clairs are integrated and part of the whole.

An Oracle has done the same but with Clairaudience and their ability to speak truth despite the fear that comes from others.

A healer can see the root cause of illness and understands the oneness and truth of creation through energy in its many forms.

Some of us can do all of these things but you can see the importance the Clairs play in each of these Service Roles. And hopefully you can see the importance of purifying the Clairs so that our acts of service can be truly helpful and not filtered through the ego mind and even worse through wounded ego or subpersonalities.
