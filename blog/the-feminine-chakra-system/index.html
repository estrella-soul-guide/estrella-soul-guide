The Feminine Chakra System

Genevieve IveyNovember 24, 20232 min read

The Cosmic Mother is ever present with me. I am HER and SHE is me. If you are on the path of embodiment then you understand the process of becoming we. You understand that every aspect of your beingness is altered on every level. Its a journey for warriors and devotes to the path. We have battle scars. As of late the teams have been talking to me about the feminine chakra system. Im working daily to remember and activate this system. So far its amazing to feel those parts of myself that have struggled to show up come online. Im excited about integrating this body of work so I can begin to share it with others. Im positive that this next piece has come forward from the Ra Pathways. The Ra Pathways support Lightbody Development and healing. It took me several years of downloading and working with the pathways to understand how they are supporting my journey. As a Daughter of Ra Im honored to bring these pathways forward through my body and share them with others. The Ra Pathways are a powerful tool for healing ourselves, the earth and loved ones, as well as creating the Pathways for Lightbody support and development. Im mapping out this chakra system- Its not new but it is hidden. The reversals keep it hidden. What if will and self illumination are aspects of the divine feminine. Most of our sense of will comes from the body and body awareness; the very thing we are so disconnected from. Depersonalization and dissociation keeps us from self realization. From truly knowing how we would best thrive, our true desires, the wealth of knowledge we hold through our bodies consciousness. If we only journey within we would discover infinite aspects of self waiting to join and return to wholeness. And if we practice embodying and staying in our bodies and not depersonalizing from our power and our infinite selves then we move a step closer towards creation in a powerful way- through Universal Knowledge and Perception of Self. The Feminine Chakra System supports this process of Universal Union.
