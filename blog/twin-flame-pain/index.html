Twin Flame Pain

Genevieve IveyOctober 9, 20244 min read

Why is the Twin Flame Journey so painful?

Channeled from Venus by Genevieve

Venus Stargate for Twin Flames, Divine Counterparts, Divine Pairs, Divine Rays, Angelics and Starseeds on the Mission for The New Templates of Love

The Twin Flame journey is a mission to anchor new templates of Love within the false matrix. Why? Because this will collectively shift humanity into their Soul Essence. We have agreed to feel the pain of the collective. To experience the fallen masculine and fallen feminine frequencies of our ancestors, to heal and harmonize this darkness that resides in every human being because of what has occurred collectively on the planet. Our beloved Virgo (Earth) has suffered from the low vibration of love enacted day to day on Earth. Twins have come to shift that by experiencing that very frequency of false love themselves. This pain is the collective pain of humanity. The pain is so intense on the journey because it creates enough force to wake you up. You are awakened through the force of tremendous pain.

Of course its not a True love that awakens you. You are still asleep when the "Twin" awakens you through rejection. The twin is the lover of the ego. Who you are after your dark night would never want the "Twin". True Divine pairs equally desire each other and have mission work together. The "Twin" wakes you up. Awakens your Kali. Awakens you to the false matrix.

Separation

Twins meet and a Soul Merge begins that activates both twins.

When twins can not be together physically, it is excruciatingly painful. It is a hallmark of this journey. In fact it is the pain that breaks you and throws you into a Dark Night of the Soul.

Unrequited love

Not knowing if your twin loves you or in fact being told by your twin that they do not love you is absolutely Soul shattering. You finally find "the one" and the soul bond is rejected by one twin. Of course its all part of the illusion that you are awakening within.

Triggering

Our twin knows exactly how to trigger all of our deepest wounds and fears. They are a catalyst for our awakening. They will launch us into our Awakening. The healing that has to be done is a side note unless the the awakening "Twin" hasn't started thier healing work. Healing is part of the illusion. Though it does help with the pain of awakening.

Fear

The fears one has to face on this journey seem to hit you out of nowhere. It's fear that is amplified by the Twin Flame energies. The intensity of the fear can cause anxiety and even panic attacks.

Surrender

Resistance to what is and what the soul is forcing the twins to experience in order to align with the Twin Flame Journey. Acceptance that you are a Divine Counterpart is extremely important. We fight the connection to the twin and the energies that are so constant and overwhelming. But the resistance actually causes more pain. We eventually have to accept that we are a Twin and we can not break the connection even if one Twin runs or denys the connection the journey continues. On the divine counterpart path we are forced to let go of what the soul requires one to let go of. It's all part of the Ego Death. The connection is also part of the illusion you are awakening from.

Longing

The longing for the other is so strong. It can take you by surprise, like a wave that washes over you. Before you know it you are spiraling into the abyss of longing for your soul's perfect partner. The longing feels soul deep. Its coming from the deepest part of your subconscious ego. It comes from deep inside of you to call out for your twin. When we are in no contact with our twin the loneliness feels like it can drown you. It subsides when we move into our souls consciouness and we start to dissolve the ego mind.

The Twin Flame Journey is the most difficult initiation you will experience in your life. You don't have to suffer alone. Create a safe space for yourself and be gentle and kind to yourself. I offer many services that can help you on the journey and bring peace and comfort. Spiritual Alchemy and Regression Therapy can help ease the intense emotions and energies that we experience on this Soul Path.

The Alchemical Path

What we call the Twin Flame Journey can look like many different paths. Different Twins have different experiences. There are signatures of this journey however and the journey persists over many lifetimes. Only very Advanced Souls choose this path as it is excruciating and requires a warriors heart. This path will however lead you to Bliss, Unconditional Love and the MISSION For LOVE ~ TRUTH AND JUSTICE FOR ALL BEINGS through your Soul's True NATURE

With Venus Love,
Genevieve
