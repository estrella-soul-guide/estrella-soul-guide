Water Initiation

Water is what is pumping in your veins ~ flowing with information.
~ The Cosmic Mother

This Ceremony is about changing that information. In this ceremony your blood is purified in the water. By reinforming the blood we change what is informing the roots of your ego mind. You are being nourished with the truth of Estrella.

The wisdom of the stars informs the water we work in ~ connecting you back to the universal truth of what you are. This Ceremony is a Mystery within itself and there it will stay ~ untouched by ego. You do not get to explain this experience away. It will always be with your soul. Because it is in your blood. You are remembering your true NATURE.
