My Timeline

A Soul’s path of remembering

  1. 1978

    Born in 1978 at the Glory Barn in Winona Lake, Indiana into a fundamentalist Christian household. A Christian Cult known as Faith Assembly, led by Hobart Freeman.

  2. Childhood

    Grew up in Dallas, Texas as a highly sensitive child. I was very empathic and had a powerful ability to create magical, mischievous worlds of play and adventure. Worlds that were very much forbidden in my fundamentalist christian upbringing.

  3. Age 10

    At the age of 10 my life became extremely challenging because of my strict abusive household. I started feeling a strong pull to my spiritual nature and away from the God of fear my family worshiped. Orbs started visiting me regularly as well as tortured spirits that had crossed over. The beginning of both light and dark manifesting in my physical world from the spiritual planes.

  4. Age 11

    At the age of 11 my mother had a complete breakdown and me and my younger sister and brother were sent to live in different households with relatives we barely knew.

  5. Teenage years

    As a Teenager my gifts started coming online in a very powerful way through dreams, visions and clairaudience. It was beautiful and dark. Both Terrifying and Confirming. I was constantly told I was experiencing demons and I needed to repent and pray. I was both curious of what I was experiencing and also fearful because of my conditioning.

  6. Age 18

    At the age of 18 I was visited by a new being of light. A Faerie. I could see its entire body all of the details of its face, hands, feet and facets of light that were also part of its physical makeup. This changed everything for me. I became enthralled with the world of Faerie and understanding why I could see this realm.

  7. Glastonbury

    I was called to Glastonbury England, Where everything would start to come together for me. I would start to remember Avalon and the sisterhood, the Christic codes and my soul’s promise to the Mother of all of Life.

  8. Age 23

    I was still confused and fearful and by the age of 23 I was married with two children and living a conventional life that I would never fit into.

  9. Age 23

    At 23 I became very ill and could not get out of bed. I was walking into doors and walls. I couldn’t stop crying. I was so fatigued I could barely function. My husband was not compassionate or concerned. I was made to feel like something was wrong with me for being sick. Finally after months I went to the Dr. and was diagnosed with Hashimoto’s. An autoimmune disease that attacks the Thyroid.

  10. The call

    I began to feel torn between my spiritual nature and my conventional life. I started drinking and rebelling. I had severe anxiety and constant panic attacks. My soul was calling me and I was doing my best to ignore the call.
