Becoming a Channel

Who am I?

A question I was catapulted into after a spontaneous spiritual experience. My experiences were so intense that I was compelled by a higher power to understand my soul.

A soul journey is not an egoic experience. It is the journey to the beyond, to your infinite self, to purpose in service to the All. It is a call so strong, so powerful that you are compelled to let go of everything you think you know and just experience yourself, your world in every moment without the illusion and distortion of the perceived self.

I have always been spiritual, psychic and connected but I was also asleep and in my illusion of self. This is the Human experience to live in the dream. To forget everything you are and live life as if your reality and story is everything that you are.

But what happens when you start to awaken from the dream? What happens when you start to remember who you are beyond this body? When you start to remember that it's all a false reality. A False Matrix.

Estrella is the Journey of the Reclamation of your Soul as a lived and embodied experience. Estrella is Embodied Star Gnosis. The remembered and embodied truth of creation. Estrella is the return to your Symbiotic relationship with all of creation.

My story of returning to my Soul is extensive. I could write book after book about Awakening, Kundalini, the Cosmic Mother, Angelics and Elementals, Kingdoms of Light, Gods and Goddesses. I've experienced initiation with them all. Because its the world I am from as a Soul. Its my Home.

I am on a journey of living as one who is ever new and ancient all at once.

Dragon wisdom

For me my journey is with God as the Creator and the many Realms of creation all remembered, felt, seen and known in this body now.

Embodiment and Purification as a daily Practice, Letting go ~ Free Falling~Surrender~Journeying into the Abyss of Self ~Dissolving Mind~ Cracking Open again and again. Purging the Distortions of Ancestors and living the truth of the path cutter. There is no separation in my life between my Soul Journey and who I am EVERYDAY.

I am my Soul, I am my Body

How did I get here? I died 1,000 deaths and I rebirthed myself over and over all while owning a business, running a home and birthing children. My life as been filled with trials and immense pain and loss. For years I was on the healing path. Submersed in the never ending journey of healing the wounded self.

I have 20 years of certifications in endless modalities that help us heal on this journey. I have worked with 10's of thousands of clients facilitating deep soul healing from the atrocities of life on this planet. I have heard and held space for the darkest and deepest of wounds.

But everything changed when I was plummeted into total Darkness.

For 8 months I couldn't breathe without immense emotional, physical and mental pain. I could barely function and my existential world was collapsing and turning on me like a rabid dog.

I was dying while still being alive. Not a ceremonial death or shedding of skin. All of me was dying. Overnight, the death dissolved 20 years of healing work and everything I thought I knew about myself, my journey into soul, what I knew of God. Everything was gone and there was only darkness.

What I didn't know then was that God was delivering me into the arms of Shakti and she was shattering my illusion. I was in Kundalini Awakening. The great Alchemical Mystery. Not a partial rising of Kundalini. Full Awakening ~ Full Death of Self as I had experienced it~ Full extraction from the False Matrix. I was returned to the womb of the Cosmic Mother to be dissolved and reborn while still being alive.

Who am I?

I am a Cosmic Breath.
I am Estrella.

With Love for your Journey to your True Nature~
